Japan is a wonderful destination for families, with safe streets, fun activities, and sights that spark kids' imaginations.

## Tokyo Disneyland & DisneySea

A magical stop for kids of all ages, with attractions found nowhere else in the world.

## Osaka Aquarium

One of the largest aquariums in the world, home to whale sharks and playful otters.

## Nara's Friendly Deer Park

Kids love feeding the wild, freely-roaming deer that wander through Nara Park.
